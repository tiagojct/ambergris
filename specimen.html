04 · status

Severity without hue

Three independent channels carry severity, so no two states share a signature. Squint, print it, or view it in greyscale: the ranking survives all three.

Neutral
1px · solid · 3% fill

Informational only. The lightest border in the set and the only one that never takes a weight above a hairline.

Success
1.5px · solid · 3% fill

Heavier edge than neutral, same quiet fill. Success should be legible without demanding attention, since nothing needs doing.

Warning
1.5px · dashed · 6% fill

The only dashed edge in the system. A broken line reads as unresolved even at a glance, which is precisely what a warning means.

Critical
3px · solid · 10% fill

Double the weight of anything else and the darkest accent on the page. Reserve it for states a person must resolve before continuing.

05 · accent

One teal, and only for interaction

Hue 190, sixty-four degrees off the greys. It marks what is current, what is a link, and what is selected. It never marks severity, and it never appears as decoration.

Accent underlines sit at accent-500 while the text itself is accent-600, which keeps the rule quieter than the word. On hover both move to accent-700 and the underline takes the text colour. Select this paragraph to see the selection fill.

accent surface

Editorial note

An accent-tinted surface with a 3px accent rule. This is the heaviest the accent is ever allowed to be in the interface, and it carries no severity meaning.

Accent belongs here

Current nav item, links, text selection, the rule on a highlighted block, focus-adjacent affordances that are about where you are.

Accent stays out

Status chips, primary buttons, chart series, icons that are not links, headings, borders on ordinary cards, anything decorative.

06 · data

The sweep, confined to charts

Five analogous hues from teal to indigo. Because they sit on one hue path they can only encode order, never category. Two variants exist because the supplied colours are mid-dark and disappear against a dark ground; pick by chart background, not by page theme.

07 · type

Six levels of grey, six jobs

Call me Ishmael.

Lead paragraph, set in text-secondary at 6.03 against surface. Long enough to introduce a section without competing with the display line above it.

Body text sits at text-primary, 16:1 against surface. In a system with no hue, weight and size do all the work that colour normally does, so the body face has to hold its own at small sizes across long passages.

Supporting copy drops to text-secondary. Still passes AA, still comfortable, clearly subordinate.

09 · elevation

Shallow on purpose

Monochrome interfaces read depth through edge contrast, not blur radius. The shadows are cool-tinted and small; the rule tokens carry most of the separation.
